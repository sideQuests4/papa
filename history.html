Company History

58 Years of Continuous Excellence

Corporate Timeline

1968

Company Founded

The Anish Doctor Corporation officially began operations. The founder entered the market with unlimited potential, zero experience, and a very very flat back-of-the-head.

Initial investors Ashok Doctor and Malti Doctor approved the project despite there being no proven track record. Senior employee Mona Doctor (now Somani) was appointed Head of Big Sister Operations and has maintained that role ever since.

1988

Architecture Division Established

The company entered the architecture industry, where it continues to produce thoughtful designs, solve problems, and yell at plumbers.


A lifelong partnership with Shivprasad was established.

1995

Strategic Partnership Formed

A landmark merger resulted in the formation of the Doctor Household. Shareholder confidence reached an all-time high.

1998

Family Operations Division Launched

Promoted to Chief Executive Dad. Responsibilities expanded dramatically, including midnight cricket, hair combing, scooter rides, life advice, airport pickups, and unlimited back scratches.


Financial reports showed a temporary decline in cash reserves due to major investments in education, clothing, books, birthday celebrations, and countless other expenses. Management unanimously declared the investment to be the company's greatest success. Long-term returns expected. Any day now. We hope.

Family unit.

2026

Official Website Released

The founder finally received the worldwide recognition he had clearly deserved.

Language Expansion Initiative

Continuous Learning
