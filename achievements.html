Awards & Recognition

Lifetime Achievement in Food Waste Management

Chief Food Recovery Officer (CFRO)

Presented in recognition of extraordinary dedication to ensuring that no edible item is ever left behind. For decades, the recipient has voluntarily accepted unfinished meals from family members, transforming potential food waste into personal responsibility.


Company records indicate that thousands of rotis, spoonfuls of rice, unfinished sabzis, and "I'm too full, you finish it" situations have been successfully resolved.


This remarkable commitment has prevented countless leftovers from meeting an untimely end in the dustbin.

Pictured in action.

Distinguished Service Medal for Vehicle Loyalty

Elite Parallel Parking Certification

Awarded for demonstrating unwavering loyalty to a single company vehicle for an astonishing number of years.

While industry standards encourage frequent upgrades, the recipient firmly believes that true friendships are built over decades. The vehicle has officially progressed from "transportation" to "honorary family member."

The committee further recognizes exceptional parking abilities, repeatedly fitting the company vehicle into spaces that witnesses unanimously described as "absolutely impossible."

Excellence in Toothpaste Cap Compliance

Lifetime Chairperson, Bathroom Standards Committee

Presented in recognition of decades of unwavering dedication to promoting proper toothpaste cap closure across all company facilities.

The recipient has personally conducted numerous educational campaigns directed primarily at one employee (his daughter), emphasizing that toothpaste tubes are, in fact, designed to be closed after use.

Despite repeated training sessions, reminder notices, and occasional strongly worded verbal feedback, compliance levels have historically remained... inconsistent.
